## What this document is
The official **"Key Features" / highlights** summary of Union Budget 2026-27 -
the at-a-glance distillation of the Budget's headline numbers and announcements.
*Specific figures are point-in-time; cross-check against the current budget
documents.*

## What to extract from a "Key Features" sheet
The analytically important headlines are always:
- **Fiscal math:** fiscal deficit (% of GDP) and its glide path, total
  receipts/expenditure, and the **market borrowing programme** (gross/net G-sec).
- **Capex:** the **capital-expenditure** number and growth - the most
  market-watched line for cyclicals and infrastructure.
- **Tax changes:** direct-tax slabs/relief and corporate/MAT changes;
  customs/indirect changes by sector.
- **Sector allocations:** infrastructure, defence, railways, roads, agriculture,
  health, education, and any flagship schemes.

## The recurring policy thrust
Consistent themes: **capex-led growth, fiscal consolidation, manufacturing /
Make in India and PLI-type support, MSME and middle-class support, and ease of
doing business / tax simplification.** These shape which sectors get tailwinds.

## Why it matters for positioning
The deficit and borrowing drive **bonds and the rupee**; the capex number and its
allocation drive **infra, capital goods, cement, defence, railways**; tax/customs
changes create discrete **sector winners and losers**. The market reaction is
about the surprise vs expectations, especially on the deficit and capex.

## How an SME should use it
Use the highlights to quickly read **deficit vs glide path, capex direction, and
sectoral tax/spend changes**, then translate to positioning: capex-heavy,
consolidation-consistent budgets favour infra/cyclicals and contain yields;
tax/customs tweaks flag specific sector trades. Treat the themes as durable and
confirm exact numbers from the live documents.
